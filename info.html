Sizing

Every listing has two measurements, taken with the shirt laid flat:

  • Pit to pit — straight across the chest, 1 inch below the armpits.
  • Length — from the highest point of the shoulder to the hem.

Vintage Japanese tees often run one size smaller than modern US sizing. The easiest way to find your size: lay a T-shirt you love flat, measure it, and compare.

Condition

These shirts are decades old. Fading, cracking and small marks are part of their story — every flaw is photographed and written in the listing.

  • A — Excellent. Little to no wear.
  • B — Good. Normal vintage wear: fading, light marks or pilling.
  • C — Fair. Visible flaws such as stains or small holes, priced accordingly.

Returns

If a shirt arrives in a condition that is not as described, contact me within 14 days and I will make it right with a refund or return.

Because every piece is one of a kind, returns for size or change of mind are accepted only if the shirt is unworn and returned within 14 days, with return shipping paid by the buyer.

FAQ

Are these official?

Each listing says where the shirt came from — official merch, event goods, staff-only shirts or prizes. If I am not sure, I say so.

Do you restock?

No. Every shirt is one of one. Once it is sold, it is gone. Subscribe to hear about the next drop.

Are the shirts washed?

Yes, gently washed and air-dried before shipping. Wash cold and hang dry to keep the print alive.

Can you find a specific shirt?

Send me a message. I cannot promise anything, but I keep a list and check it every week.

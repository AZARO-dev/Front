Photo — the shop / the owner

Sorting a new batch, Koenji, Tokyo.

Saihōsō (再放送) means “rerun” — an old episode, broadcast again. That is what this shop is: anime T-shirts that already had one life in Japan, getting a second one with you.

rerun is run by one person in Tokyo. Every week I go through thrift stores, recycle shops, flea markets and the occasional warehouse clear-out, looking for shirts that were made while the shows were still on air — theatrical promos, fan club mail-order goods, event shirts, magazine prizes and studio staff tees.

Most of these were never sold outside Japan. Many were never sold at all. I photograph, measure and describe each one honestly, flaws included, and ship it to you myself.

Thank you for giving them a new home.

How it works

  1. Found in Japan. Sourced by hand, never from bulk resellers.
  2. Checked and washed. Gently washed, inspected and graded A / B / C.
  3. Dropped on Fridays. New pieces go up every Friday at 8pm JST.
  4. Shipped from Tokyo. Packed by hand and sent with tracking, worldwide.
